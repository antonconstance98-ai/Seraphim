# Phase 03.1 Plan 01: Parallel External Research Tracks Summary

**One-liner:** 4 focused parallel Perplexity research agents (solutions, anti-patterns, architecture, dependencies) replace the single external research track in the Discover phase.

## What Was Built

The Discover phase (Wing I) external research track was redesigned from a single monolithic research step into 4 parallel focused agents. Each agent covers a distinct research dimension without bleeding into others, maximising breadth and depth of web research.

### Task 1: discover.md Step 6 replaced with Steps 6a-6e

- **Step 6a** — Resolve external model (same logic as before, now labelled)
- **Step 6b** — Define all 4 track prompts with focus directives
- **Step 6c** — Execute tracks in parallel (Task tool for Opus inline; Bash `&` + `wait` for dispatch path)
- **Step 6d** — Handle individual track failures with `emitTrackFailed()` stubs — tracks fail independently
- **Step 6e** — Merge all 4 outputs into `external.md` with PHASE_START/PHASE_COMPLETE envelope and per-track TRACK_START/TRACK_COMPLETE markers

Steps 1-5 and 7-9 of discover.md are unchanged. The internal track (Step 7) is fully unaffected.
