A fallback event is a record where BOTH conditions are true:
  1. source === 'api-fallback'  (set by codex-handoff.js when Codex CLI fails)

`fallbackCost` = sum of `cost_usd` for all records matching BOTH conditions above. This is the full
actual cost of MiniMax fallback executions, not incremental cost above Codex (Codex failed and
produced no cost for these records, so the full MiniMax cost IS the additional cost).
